Harvest Festivals

Harvest festivals are important celebrations that mark the harvesting of crops and express gratitude for nature, farmers and the agricultural community. These festivals are celebrated in different parts of India according to local seasons, crops and traditions. They bring families and communities together through prayers, traditional food, cultural activities and celebrations.
