Losar Festival

Tibetan New Year Festival

About Losar

Losar is the traditional Tibetan New Year festival celebrated by Tibetan Buddhist communities. In India, it is especially celebrated in regions such as Ladakh, Sikkim, Himachal Pradesh and Arunachal Pradesh. The festival marks the beginning of a new year and is celebrated with prayers, cultural activities and family gatherings.

Before Losar, homes are cleaned and decorated as part of preparations for the new year. People visit monasteries and offer prayers for peace, happiness and prosperity. Buddhist monks perform traditional prayers and ceremonies in monasteries. Colourful decorations, prayer flags and traditional costumes add to the beauty of the celebrations.

During Losar, families gather together and enjoy traditional food. People wear traditional clothes, exchange greetings and visit relatives and friends. Traditional music, dances and cultural performances are also organized in many places. Losar is an important festival that represents Tibetan Buddhist culture, traditions and community values.

Special Foods

Momos

Momos are popular dumplings enjoyed during celebrations in Himalayan regions.

Thukpa

Thukpa is a warm noodle soup popular in Tibetan and Himalayan cuisine.

Tingmo

Tingmo is a soft steamed bread commonly served with traditional dishes.
