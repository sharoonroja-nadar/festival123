Bhagoria Festival

Colourful Tribal Festival of Madhya Pradesh

About Bhagoria

Bhagoria is a colourful tribal festival celebrated mainly in the Malwa and Nimar regions of Madhya Pradesh. It is associated especially with the Bhil and Bhilala communities. The festival is held around the time of Holi and is known for its lively village fairs, traditional music, dancing and colourful clothing.

During Bhagoria, people gather in local markets and fairs to celebrate with their families and communities. Traditional musical instruments are played and people take part in folk dances and cultural activities. The markets are decorated with colourful items, handicrafts and local products. The festival provides an opportunity for communities to come together and enjoy their traditional culture.

Traditional dress, ornaments, folk songs and dances are important features of Bhagoria. Local foods and snacks are also prepared and enjoyed during the celebrations. The festival reflects the rich cultural heritage of the tribal communities of Madhya Pradesh. Bhagoria is therefore an important cultural celebration that brings together people, traditions, music and local customs.

Special Foods

Poha is a simple and popular breakfast dish made from flattened rice.
