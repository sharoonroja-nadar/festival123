Wangala Festival

Harvest Festival of Meghalaya

About Wangala

Wangala is an important harvest festival celebrated mainly by the Garo community of Meghalaya and parts of the northeastern region of India. It is also known as the Hundred Drums Festival. The festival is celebrated after the harvest and is a time to express gratitude for a successful agricultural season. It is filled with traditional music, dancing and colourful cultural performances.

The most famous feature of Wangala is the traditional drum performances. Groups of dancers and musicians perform together while playing traditional drums called nagara and other instruments. Men and women wear colourful traditional clothes and ornaments during the celebrations. The rhythmic music and group dances create a lively and joyful atmosphere.

Wangala is an important celebration of Garo culture and community life. Traditional songs, dances, food and customs are shared during the festival. People gather with family members and friends and participate in cultural activities. The festival helps preserve and showcase the traditional heritage of the Garo people and the rich culture of Meghalaya.
