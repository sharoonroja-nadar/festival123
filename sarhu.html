Sarhul Festival

Festival of Nature and Spring

About Sarhul Festival

Sarhul is an important tribal festival celebrated mainly in Jharkhand and nearby regions of eastern and central India. It is especially associated with tribal communities such as the Oraon, Munda and Ho. The festival celebrates nature, the arrival of spring and the beginning of a new agricultural season. It is an important part of the cultural traditions of these communities.

During Sarhul, people worship nature and offer prayers for the well-being of their families and communities. Sal flowers have an important place in the celebrations because they bloom around this time of year. Traditional rituals are performed at the village sacred grove, and people seek blessings for good rainfall, healthy crops and prosperity.

Sarhul is also celebrated with traditional songs, dances and music. People wear colourful traditional clothes and gather together in villages to participate in the celebrations. Traditional food is prepared and shared with family members and friends. The festival reflects the close relationship between tribal communities, nature, agriculture and their traditional way of life.

Special Foods

Rice

Rice is an important staple food served during traditional celebrations.

Dal

Dal is commonly served with rice as part of a traditional meal.

Pitha

Pitha is a traditional rice-based food enjoyed during festive occasions.
