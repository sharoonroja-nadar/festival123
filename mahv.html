Maha Shivaratri Festival

About Maha Shivaratri

Maha Shivaratri is one of the important Hindu festivals dedicated to Lord Shiva. The name Maha Shivaratri means the Great Night of Shiva. Devotees observe prayers, fasting and meditation on this special day. Temples dedicated to Lord Shiva are beautifully decorated and devotees visit temples to offer prayers.

Maha Shivaratri is celebrated on the fourteenth day of the dark fortnight of the month of Phalguna according to the Hindu calendar. Devotees believe that worshipping Lord Shiva on this night brings peace, devotion and spiritual strength.

How Maha Shivaratri is Celebrated

Devotees observe fasting and stay awake during the night. They offer milk, water, fruits and Bilva leaves to the Shiva Lingam. Many temples conduct special prayers and Abhishekam. People chant prayers and devotional songs dedicated to Lord Shiva.
