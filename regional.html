Regional Festivals

Regional festivals are special celebrations connected with the culture, traditions and history of particular regions of India. Different states and communities celebrate these festivals in their own unique ways. Regional festivals bring people together through traditional food, music, dance, prayers, art and cultural activities. They help preserve the rich cultural diversity and traditions of India.
