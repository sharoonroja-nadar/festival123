Traditional Festivals

Traditional festivals are an important part of India's rich cultural heritage. They are celebrated with traditional customs, music, dance, food, clothing and community activities. Different regions of India have their own unique festivals and traditions. These celebrations bring families and communities together and help preserve India's diverse cultural heritage for future generations.
