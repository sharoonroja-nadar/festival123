Religious Festivals

Religious festivals are important celebrations observed by different communities across India. They are connected with faith, religious traditions, stories and cultural practices. People celebrate these festivals by visiting places of worship, offering prayers, preparing traditional food and spending time with family and community members. These festivals also reflect India's rich cultural and religious diversity.
