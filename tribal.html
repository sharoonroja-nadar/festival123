Tribal Festivals

Tribal festivals are traditional celebrations observed by indigenous communities in different parts of India. These festivals are closely connected with nature, agriculture, community life and ancestral traditions. People celebrate them through traditional songs, dances, music, food, prayers and cultural activities. These festivals help preserve the rich heritage and traditions of tribal communities.
